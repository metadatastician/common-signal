= Common Signal governance review form

This is a facilitation template, not evidence that a review occurred. Do not put
names, membership status, confidential casework, source material, or sensitive
workplace details into the public repository.

== Decision boundary

* What may this review change?
* What is outside this review and why?
* Is the outcome still genuinely open?
* Is any employer, funder, vendor, or commissioning-body influence relevant?

== Findings

* What accurately represents work as done?
* What work, risk, group, power relationship, or collective right is missing?
* Which terms, categories, filters, or visualisations could cause harm?
* Which claims or inferences must the system refuse?
* Are the prohibited-use rules adequate in every mandatory adversarial scenario?
* Can affected people and representatives inspect, challenge, stop, correct, and
  obtain remedy?
* What must change before support is possible?
* What reservations or dissent must remain visible?
