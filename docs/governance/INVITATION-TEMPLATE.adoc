== What Common Signal is

Common Signal is an experimental, worker-centred public-interest project about
the effects of AI on journalism, public relations, communications, and related
media work. It currently uses synthetic examples only. It is not an NUJ service
and is not endorsed by the NUJ or another union.

== What we are asking

We invite you to examine and change the project's draft governance rules,
including prohibited uses, consultation records, evidence limits, challenge and
remedy, and the conditions that would apply before any live data source could
even be proposed. The outcome is open: participants may require changes, impose
conditions, decline support, or recommend that work stop.

This invitation seeks: _[personal experience / workplace review / recognised
union review / another stated mandate]_. Please do not imply a collective or
union mandate unless the relevant body has actually provided one.

== How comments affect the project

Required changes, reservations and dissent will be mapped to a published
response without attribution unless permission is explicit. Participants will
receive their proposed record by _[date]_ and may correct it through _[route]_.
Project maintainers cannot turn individual comments into a claim of collective
approval. Any later collective decision must follow the authorised body's own
process and record its precise scope, conditions, review date and withdrawal
route.
