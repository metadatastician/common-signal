= Worker governance and collective remedy
:toc:

== Status and authority

This is an executable project contract, not a collective agreement and not a
claim of NUJ or trade-union endorsement. It establishes defaults for the
experimental implementation until appropriately authorised worker and union
participants replace or ratify them through a real governance process.

The process, mandate boundaries, review form, adversarial scenarios, and
ratification threshold are specified in
`docs/governance/CO-DESIGN-PROTOCOL.adoc`. Current machine-readable status is
`draft-not-consulted`; creation of consultation materials does not change it.
The loopback service exposes that same embedded record at `/v1/governance` and
an accessible explanation at `/governance`; these are transparency surfaces,
not participation or voting mechanisms.

== Governing principle

People affected by collection, classification, comparison, or publication must
have meaningful power over purpose, scope, safeguards, interpretation,
retention, challenge, and remedy. Individual consent cannot be used to bypass a
recognised collective process or to legitimise structural workplace monitoring.

== Consultation is a process, not a boolean

Common Signal records a staged process:

. proposed change;
. information shared with affected workers or representatives;
. consultation open while outcomes remain changeable;
. collective negotiation;
. agreement reached;
. disputed process or outcome;
. review due.

`not-recorded` means only that the available evidence does not establish the
stage. It must not be rendered or analysed as “consultation did not occur.”

Participation routes distinguish direct consultation, recognised-union
representation, elected worker bodies, mixed arrangements, and absence from the
record. A consultation record carries its scope, evidence reference, worker
body where appropriate, and unresolved matters.

== Purpose limitation

The current implementation recognises four possible high-level purposes:

* public-interest research;
* worker-led research;
* evidence for consultation or collective bargaining;
* public-accountability reporting.

A declared permitted purpose does not override a prohibited processing
characteristic. `policies/prohibited-uses.json` is default-deny and has no
override mechanism.

== Prohibited uses

The executable Rust policy rejects:

* identifiable-worker performance scoring;
* disciplinary, dismissal, redundancy-selection, or adverse-employment use;
* union membership, representation, organising, or activity surveillance;
* monitoring industrial action or preparation for it;
* inference about workforce, union, or membership sentiment or legitimacy;
* unreviewed automated editorial or professional decisions.

These prohibitions apply regardless of whether the requester describes a wider
project as research, efficiency, safety, innovation, or public interest.

== Context-specific duties

Journalism, PR, and communications are related but non-interchangeable
professional contexts. The canonical profiles preserve distinct duties and
risks, including editorial independence and source protection in journalism;
authorship, client pressure, and availability intensification in PR; and
accessibility, public accountability, and centralised message control in
communications.

Cross-media records must retain those component duties rather than imposing one
sector's performance measures on another.

== Challenge and remedy

The versioned challenge schema supports submitted, under-review, corrected,
upheld, rejected-with-reasons, and unresolved states. It records whether the
challenger has collective representation and the remedy or next step.

A production process will also need, before live data collection:

* independent escalation and appeal;
* collectively agreed response times;
* correction and deletion propagation;
* safe routes for confidential and represented challenges;
* non-retaliation commitments;
* audit access for authorised worker representatives;
* enforceable consequences for prohibited reuse.

== Current privacy and retention boundary

The current service uses authored synthetic fixtures only. It stores no requests,
identifiers, analytics, or challenge records. The HTTP responses send
`Cache-Control: no-store`. Live connectors remain prohibited until lawful basis,
data minimisation, copyright, retention, deletion, security, and collective
governance are specified and tested.
