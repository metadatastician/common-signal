!

Inference boundary

This evidence can describe the observed workflows. It cannot estimate national prevalence, prove causation, or predict effects in another organisation.

Challenge status

One unresolved methodological note: participants may change checking behaviour while observed.

Connector architecture

Sources remain distinct

Social expressions, journalism, search interest, surveys, workflow observations and incidents answer different questions. The system does not average them into one index.
